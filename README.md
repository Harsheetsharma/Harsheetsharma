## 🏗️ Engineering Philosophy

I believe good backend systems should be designed for **correctness**, **resilience**, and **predictability** before they are optimized for scale.

### Principles I Follow

- ✅ Correctness over cleverness
- ⚙️ Reliability before scalability
- 🔄 Deterministic behavior under concurrency
- 🛡️ Explicit failure handling over hidden assumptions
- 📊 Design for observability, debugging, and recovery
- 📚 Document architectural decisions and trade-offs
- 🧩 Build systems that are easy to extend and maintain

---

> Good backend engineering isn't just about making requests succeed.

It's about understanding what happens when:

- 💥 Databases become unavailable
- 📦 Queues start backing up
- 👷 Workers crash unexpectedly
- 🌐 Networks partition or latency spikes
- 🔁 Clients retry the same request multiple times
- ⚠️ Services fail in the middle of a distributed workflow

Designing for those scenarios is what makes systems reliable—not just feature complete.
